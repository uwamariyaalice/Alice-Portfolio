About Me

I am a motivated Business Information Technology graduate from the University of Rwanda with a genuine passion for bridging business and technology. I believe that the best digital solutions are built by people who understand both the technical side and the human side of a problem.

My journey started with a strong foundation in information systems and business processes. I later expanded my skills into frontend development, earning a professional certificate for building the World Star School Management System using React.js, Next.js, and Tailwind CSS. Today, I am also growing my expertise in cybersecurity to make the systems I build safer and more reliable.

Beyond code, I am a strong communicator and fast learner. I enjoy working with people, understanding their needs, and turning those needs into practical technological solutions that create real value.
